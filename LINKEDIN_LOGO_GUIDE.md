# Hearthside Works LinkedIn Logo Guide

## Logo Files Created

### Profile Logo (Square)
- **File**: `hearthside-linkedin-logo.svg` and `hearthside-linkedin-logo-300.png`
- **Size**: 300x300px (LinkedIn recommended minimum)
- **Design**: White Hearthside arch on orange circle background
- **Usage**: Company profile picture

### Banner Image
- **File**: `hearthside-linkedin-banner.svg` and `hearthside-linkedin-banner-1128.png`
- **Size**: 1128x191px (LinkedIn standard banner size)
- **Design**: Orange background with arch logo, company name, and tagline
- **Usage**: Company page header banner

## LinkedIn Upload Instructions

### Profile Logo:
1. Go to your LinkedIn Company Page: https://www.linkedin.com/company/hearthside-works/
2. Click the camera icon on the profile image
3. Upload `hearthside-linkedin-logo-300.png`
4. Adjust positioning if needed

### Banner Image:
1. On your Company Page, click the camera icon on the banner area
2. Upload `hearthside-linkedin-banner-1128.png`
3. Preview and save

## Design Rationale

**Color**: Orange (#D97543) - Your brand's primary warm, inviting color
**Arch**: The Hearthside arch symbolizes connection and gathering
**White**: High contrast for visibility in LinkedIn's interface
**Typography**: Clean, professional sans-serif for readability
