**Text:**

Why I built Hearthside Works

After years in education and technology, I kept seeing the same pattern: brilliant people struggling to connect. Not for lack of trying—but because they didn't speak the same "language."

An ADHD team member would get frustrated when a neurotypical manager wanted detailed written updates.

A Gen Z employee would feel dismissed when a Boomer boss preferred phone calls.

A liberal and conservative would talk past each other, both feeling unheard.

The problem wasn't bad intentions. It was invisible differences in how our minds work.

That's why I created Clarity Coach—to make these differences visible and bridge-able.

Because when we feel heard, seen, and known, we can do our best work and build our best relationships.

What communication gap have you struggled with?

### Post 3: Product Demo (Day 5)
**Text:**
\`\`\`
How Clarity Coach works in 60 seconds ⏱️

1️⃣ Draft Mode: Write your message, select who you're talking to
2️⃣ AI analyzes your communication style and theirs
3️⃣ Get a translation that lands better—with explanations why
4️⃣ Learn patterns over time, reduce reliance on the tool

Real example:
"Can you send me the report?" 
→ Becomes: "When you have a moment, could you send the Q4 report? I'm looking specifically at the sales data for our meeting Thursday. No rush if you need until Wednesday!"

Why? Because your ADHD colleague needs:
- Clear timeline expectations
- Specific context
- Permission to manage their own schedule

Try it free: https://hearthsideworks.com/apps/clarity
