# Politically Motivated Reasoning: Identity Protection Over Truth

## Definition

**Politically motivated reasoning** (PMRP) refers to the tendency of individuals to unconsciously conform the assessment of factual information to the goal of **identity protection**: the formation of beliefs that maintain a person's status in an **affinity group united by shared values**.

## Why It's Rational

For most citizens, beliefs about policy-relevant facts (like climate change) will typically have **no meaningful impact on the risks** these issues pose or on policy adoption. In these circumstances, the reliable activation of **affective stances that convey group allegiance** is the only use most citizens have for such beliefs.

Therefore, politically motivated reasoning can be understood to be **perfectly rational** from an identity-protection standpoint.

## How It Works

PMRP involves adjusting the **weight assigned to evidence conditional on its identity congruence**. The likelihood ratio is derived from the **impact crediting the information will have on aligning beliefs with an identity-defining group**, leading to revised factual belief that **conforms to the group's predispositions**.

## Implications for Communication

- Simply presenting facts will not change minds when identity is at stake
- Partisan polarization increases when both sides see the same evidence
- Smart people are better at defending their position, not finding truth
- Group identity protection is a stronger motivator than truth-seeking
