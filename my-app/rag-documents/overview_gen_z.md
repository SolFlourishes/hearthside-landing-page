## 💡 Publication and Context Overview

[cite_start]This document synthesizes a scoping review titled "A comprehensive overview of Generation Z in the workplace"[cite: 1646]. [cite_start]The review was supported by the Beasiswa Pendidikan Indonesia (BPI)[cite: 1649].

### Gen Z Definition
* [cite_start]**Birth Years:** Individuals born roughly between **1995 and 2010** (some definitions extend to 2012)[cite: 1651].
* [cite_start]**Demographics:** Gen Z comprises more than a quarter of the US population [cite: 1653] [cite_start]and is predicted to constitute **30% of the workforce by 2030**[cite: 1654].
* **Core Characteristics:**
    * [cite_start]Often described as **'digital natives'** due to extensive engagement with technology[cite: 1655].
    * [cite_start]They are quick to respond to issues and actively find content[cite: 1656].
    * [cite_start]They are noted as being more **individualistic and happier**[cite: 1657].
    * [cite_start]Older generations sometimes describe them negatively as careless, impolite, and lazy[cite: 1657].

## 🔬 Research Purpose and Methodology

### Research Purpose
[cite_start]The study aimed to provide a comprehensive overview of Gen Z in the workplace by synthesizing results from previous research[cite: 1659]. [cite_start]The existing literature was noted as being **fragmented** and lacking a cohesive synthesis[cite: 1660].

### Methodology
* [cite_start]**Design:** A **scoping review** methodology was used to aggregate results from 11 articles[cite: 1662]. [cite_start]This approach describes key concepts and available evidence in a research area[cite: 1663].
* [cite_start]**Framework:** The study followed the foundational principles of **Arksey and O'Malley (2005)** and the **PRISMA 2020** guidelines[cite: 1664].
* [cite_start]**Data Set:** **Eleven quantitative research articles** published between 2017 and 2023 were included [cite: 1668][cite_start], covering **4,465 participants** from nine countries[cite: 1669].
* [cite_start]**Search Strategy:** The **PCC framework** (Population: Gen Z employees, Concept: workplace settings, Context: workplace) guided the systematic search [cite: 1665] [cite_start]across five major databases[cite: 1666].

## 📊 Key Findings: Central Themes and Expectations

The review identified four central themes critical to understanding Gen Z in the workplace.

| Theme | Specific Findings and Characteristics |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Gen Z Work Expectations** | [cite_start]<ul><li>Desire **transparency, meaningful engagement**, and opportunities for personal and professional development [cite: 1672][cite_start].</li><li>Desire **frequent and instantaneous feedback** [cite: 1673][cite_start].</li><li>Prioritize **work-life balance** and flexibility [cite: 1673][cite_start].</li><li>Appreciate **working independently** and seek a sense of purpose [cite: 1674][cite_start].</li><li>Value **pay, benefits, and job stability** (due to experiencing the Great Recession) [cite: 1675][cite_start].</li><li>**Unmet expectations** significantly influence the decision to stay or leave[cite: 1676].</li></ul> |
| **2. Role of Technology** | [cite_start]<ul><li>Technology plays a **crucial role in enhancing productivity** [cite: 1677][cite_start].</li><li>Skilled at using **digital tools for collaboration, communication**, multitasking, and information retrieval [cite: 1677][cite_start].</li><li>Generally receptive to technology adoption[cite: 1678].</li></ul> |
| **3. Intergenerational Challenges** | [cite_start]<ul><li>Differing expectations and values create **significant challenges** [cite: 1679][cite_start].</li><li>Gen Z emphasizes **trust and flexibility**, whereas older generations (Gen X, Baby Boomers) may prioritize hierarchy and loyalty [cite: 1679][cite_start].</li><li>Differences in communication styles (e.g., **digital reliance vs. interpersonal skills**) can lead to misunderstandings and tension[cite: 1680].</li></ul> |
| **4. Significance of Leadership Style** | [cite_start]<ul><li>Leadership style has a **significant impact** on Gen Z's attitudes and turnover decisions [cite: 1681][cite_start].</li><li>Expect **trust and respect** to be **earned** through outstanding performance, not granted automatically based on position [cite: 1682][cite_start].</li><li>Favor **transformational leadership** over transactional, financial, and sanctions-based leadership [cite: 1683][cite_start].</li><li>**Perceived supervisory support (PSS)** had the greatest impact on engaging Gen Z[cite: 1684].</li></ul> |

## ✅ Retention Drivers, Challenges, and Recommendations

### Intent to Stay or Quit
* [cite_start]**Unmet expectations** (like work flexibility and development opportunities) increase the possibility of employees leaving[cite: 1686].
* [cite_start]Feeling **overqualified** or being assigned **illegitimate tasks** diminishes their sense of perceived insider status, leading to work withdrawal and increasing the propensity to quit[cite: 1687].

### Key Engagement Drivers
Key engagement drivers include:
* [cite_start]Opportunities for **career growth** [cite: 1688]
* [cite_start]A **positive work environment** [cite: 1688]
* [cite_start]**Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)** initiatives [cite: 1688]
* [cite_start]**Supportive organizational culture** [cite: 1688]
* [cite_start]**Recognition/rewards** for contributions [cite: 1688]

### Practical Recommendations
* [cite_start]Organizations must adjust policies to prioritize **flexibility** and adopt **digital tools for collaboration**[cite: 1691].
* [cite_start]Leaders should be educated on **inclusive practices** aligned with Gen Z values[cite: 1691].
* [cite_start]Initiatives promoting **intergenerational understanding** can enhance collaboration[cite: 1692].
* [cite_start]Implement **job rotation programs** to improve skill development and align tasks with individual interests, addressing their quick loss of interest in routine tasks[cite: 1692].
* [cite_start]Provide a **supportive environment with transparent procedures and clear rewards** to improve motivation[cite: 1690].
