# Social Stories: Evidence-Based Intervention for ASD

## 💡 Definition, Origin, and Purpose

### Originator and Definition
[cite_start]Social Stories ($\text{SS}^{\text{TM}}$) were first introduced in 1993 by Carol Gray[cite: 1380]. [cite_start]They are short stories written with the goal of **objectively sharing important social information** with individuals with Autism Spectrum Disorders (ASD)[cite: 1381].
