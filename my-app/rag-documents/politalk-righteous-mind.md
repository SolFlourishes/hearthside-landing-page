# The Righteous Mind: Why Good People Are Divided

## The Struggle Between "For or Against"

Human nature is intrinsically moral, but also intrinsically **moralistic, critical, and judgmental**. The obsession with righteousness (leading to self-righteousness) is the normal human condition. The struggle between "**for or against**" is described as the **mind's worst disease**.

We are all **self-righteous hypocrites**. Wisdom requires stepping back and dropping the moralism.

## Principle 1: Intuitions Come First

**Moral intuitions arise automatically and instantaneously**, long before moral reasoning begins, and these first intuitions drive later reasoning.

### The Rider and the Elephant

- **The Elephant:** Represents the other **99 percent of mental processes** (intuition, automatic processes) that occur outside of awareness and govern most behavior
- **The Rider:** Represents **conscious reasoning**
- **The Function:** The rider's job is to **serve the elephant**. Moral reasoning is evolved to further social agendas, **justify one's own actions, and defend the teams we belong to**, rather than figure out the truth

### Motivated Reasoning

- When people *want* to believe something, they ask "Can I believe it?" and search for a single piece of pseudo-evidence to grant permission
- When people *do not* want to believe something, they ask "Must I believe it?" and search for a single reason to doubt the claim
- **IQ and Bias:** Smart people are effective lawyers, investing their IQ in **"buttressing their own case"** but are no better at finding reasons for the opposing side

### Changing Minds

To change someone's mind, one must **talk to the elephant first** to elicit new intuitions, rather than appealing solely to reason. **Empathy** is described as an antidote to righteousness.

## Principle 2: Six Moral Foundations

People in Western, Educated, Industrialized, Rich, and Democratic (WEIRD) societies are unusual in emphasizing the **ethic of autonomy** (rights, liberty, justice).

### The Six Foundations

1. **Care/Harm:** Protecting offspring and preventing suffering
2. **Fairness/Cheating:** Evolved from reciprocal altruism
3. **Liberty/Oppression:** Opposing bullies and those who dominate
4. **Loyalty/Betrayal:** Forming and maintaining political teams
5. **Authority/Subversion:** Responding to social hierarchy
6. **Sanctity/Degradation:** Avoiding pathogens and degradation

### The Conservative Advantage

- **Liberals/The Left** primarily rely on the **Care and Fairness** foundations. The most sacred value for American liberals is **caring for victims of oppression**
- **Conservatives/The Right** utilize **all six foundations** relatively equally. This broader palate gives conservative politicians more ways to connect with voters

## Principle 3: Morality Binds and Blinds

Human beings are **90 percent chimp** (selfish, competitive) and **10 percent bee** (groupish, hive-minded). Humans are groupish: we love joining teams, and our profound altruism is mostly aimed at **members of our own groups**.

### Moral Matrices

People bind themselves into political teams that share moral narratives. Each matrix is a **complete, unified, and emotionally compelling worldview**. Once bound by a matrix, people become **blind to alternative moral worlds**.

America's political class has become highly **Manichaean** (simplifying issues into clear good and evil), making compromise a "sin". Everyone goes blind when talking about their **sacred objects**.
