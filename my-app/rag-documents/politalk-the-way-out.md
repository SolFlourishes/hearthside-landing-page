# The Way Out: Escaping Toxic Polarization

## The Crisis

Toxic polarization is a **first-order problem** that is dangerous and contagious, hindering society's ability to address other existential issues. Under high threat, many feel a pull toward simplifying the world into **clear good and evil** (**Manichaean thinking**).

## The Exhausted Majority

86 percent of Americans are exhausted, miserable, and desperately seeking a way out of the culture of contempt. Extreme misery and instability are a **necessary condition for radical change**.

## The Five Modes of Practice

### 1. Reset: Capture the Power of New Beginnings

- **Emotional Reservoirs:** Establishing an emotional reservoir of rapport or positivity early is critical
- **Forward Framing:** Start by identifying the best-case scenario rather than examining grievances
- **Process Toxic Emotions:** Toxic emotions must be processed constructively *before* engaging in dialogue

### 2. Bolster and Break: Locate Latent Bubbles

- **Bolstering:** Identify and build on "positive pockets of self-healing potential" or **"bright spots"** already working effectively
- **Breaking:** Diminish the attraction of destructive propensities by **"reverse engineering"** them

### 3. Complicate: Embrace Contradictory Complexity

This is paramount for correcting the clarity and certainty derived from highly dichotomized thinking.

- When discussing hot emotional topics, presenting information simply as pro versus con can backfire
- **Complicate the narrative** by featuring **nuance, contradiction, and ambiguity**
- In high conflict, **"coherence is bad journalism, bordering on malpractice"**

### 4. Move: Activate Novel Pathways

- **Locomotion over Assessment:** Focus on moving forward toward a goal rather than critically evaluating options
- **Physical Movement:** Going for a walk, exercising, and moving in **nonurban spaces** help shift the mind
- **Synchronization:** Moving forward together physically (synchrony) helps stir empathy

### 5. Adapt: Seek Evolution for Revolution

- **Guiding Vision:** Identify a **"North Star"**—a vision for the direction you hope to travel toward
- **Ongoing Process:** Change requires considerable attention, adaptation, and adjustment over a **long, circuitous journey**

## The New Rules

**Get Ready:** See that change is possible; Stop and reset

**Change Course:** Shift focus from conflict to context; Spot what's already working; Simplify only after complicating

**Settle-In:** Synchronize movement with others; Support each other; Settle in for the long journey

The ultimate goal is to promote and sustain **political tolerance**.
