# Code of Civil Discourse: Framework for Respectful Dialogue

## Foundational Principle

The **respectful and constructive airing of different points of view is critical to successful dialogue**. Individuals who adopt the Code pledge to engage in **respectful and constructive civil discourse**.

## The Five Pledges

1. **Promoting an Inclusive Environment:** We will promote an **inclusive environment** where **diverse perspectives are shared and considered**

2. **Active Listening and Understanding:** We will **listen attentively** and **ask questions to understand** others' positions

3. **Demonstrating Respect:** We will show **respect for ideas and views presented, even when we disagree**

4. **Fair Presentation of Reasons:** We will explain our positions by **fairly presenting the reasons for them**

5. **Focusing on Salient Issues:** We will **avoid personal attacks** or **other tactics that distract attention from the salient issues**
