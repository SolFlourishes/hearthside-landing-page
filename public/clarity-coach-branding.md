# Clarity Coach Branding

## Available Files

1. **clarity-coach-logo.png** - Standard logo with transparency, rectangular format
2. **clarity-coach-logo-square.png** - Square format for social media, app stores, and OAuth providers
3. **clarity-coach-logo.svg** - Vector format for scalability

## Logo Design

The Clarity Coach logo integrates the Hearthside Works brand identity while establishing its own distinct presence.

### Design Elements

1. **Hearthside Works Arch** (Orange/Coral #E28A6D)
   - The foundational arch from the parent brand grounds the logo
   - Represents the hearth - warmth, connection, and home
   - Curves upward symbolizing support and growth

2. **Communication Speech Bubble** (Teal #007B8C)
   - Sits atop the arch, showing communication built on foundation
   - Contains three dots representing dialogue, clarity, and understanding
   - The white dots symbolize clear, illuminated communication

3. **Typography**
   - Font: Georgia (serif)
   - Professional, approachable, and readable
   - Matches Hearthside Works brand typography

## Brand Connection

The logo visually communicates that Clarity Coach is:
- **Rooted in Hearthside Works** (orange arch foundation)
- **Focused on communication** (teal speech bubble)
- **Creating clarity** (white dots of understanding)

## For LinkedIn Authentication

Use the **clarity-coach-logo-square.png** file when setting up LinkedIn authentication in Supabase:

1. Go to Supabase Dashboard → Authentication → Providers → LinkedIn
2. Upload the square logo (minimum 400x400px recommended)
3. The logo will appear on the LinkedIn OAuth consent screen
