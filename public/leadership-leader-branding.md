## Logo Design

The Leadership Leader logo integrates the Hearthside Works brand identity while establishing its own distinct presence focused on leadership and growth.

### Design Elements

1. **Hearthside Works Arch** (Orange/Coral #E28A6D)
   - The foundational arch from the parent brand grounds the logo
   - Represents the hearth - warmth, connection, and home
   - Curves upward symbolizing support and growth

2. **Leadership Growth Circle** (Green #10B981)
   - Sits atop the arch, showing leadership built on foundation
   - Contains mountain peak representing growth and achievement
   - Leader at peak with team members below showing leadership connection
   - White elements symbolize clarity and guidance

3. **Typography**
   - Font: Georgia (serif)
   - Professional, approachable, and readable
   - Matches Hearthside Works brand typography

## Brand Connection

The logo visually communicates that Leadership Leader is:
- **Rooted in Hearthside Works** (orange arch foundation)
- **Focused on growth and development** (green circle with peak)
- **Building strong leaders** (leader connected to team)

## Brand Colors

- Primary: `#10B981` (Green - Growth/Leadership)
- Secondary: `#FFFFFF` (White)
- Foundation: `#E28A6D` (Hearthside Orange)

## Logo Represents

- **Mountain Peak**: Growth, achievement, reaching new heights
- **Leader at Top**: Leadership development and guidance
- **Team Below**: Connection, mentorship, collaborative leadership
- **Clean Design**: Professional and approachable

## Design Philosophy

Leadership Leader follows the Hearthside Works design family:
- Uses the signature orange arch as foundation
- Adds a distinct primary color (green vs Clarity Coach's teal)
- Incorporates white elements for clarity and focus
- Maintains consistent typography and visual language
