# LinkedIn Image Specifications

## Files Created

### Profile Logo: `hearthside-linkedin-profile-logo.jpg`
- **Size**: 400x400px
- **Format**: JPG
- **Design**: Orange Hearthside arch centered on white background
- **Use**: Company profile picture

### Cover Image: `hearthside-linkedin-cover.jpg`
- **Size**: 1128x191px  
- **Format**: JPG
- **Design**: Orange arch logo with company name and tagline on warm gradient
- **Use**: Company page cover photo

## How to Upload

1. Go to https://www.linkedin.com/company/hearthside-works/admin/
2. **Profile Logo**: Click on the current logo → "Upload logo" → Select `hearthside-linkedin-profile-logo.jpg`
3. **Cover Image**: Click "Add a cover photo" → Select `hearthside-linkedin-cover.jpg`

## Design Notes
- Orange arch color: #E86C3C (brand primary)
- Clean, professional aesthetic
- High contrast for visibility
- Optimized for LinkedIn's display requirements
