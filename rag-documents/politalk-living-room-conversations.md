# Living Room Conversations: Structured Dialogue Guides

## Mission

The fundamental goal of Living Room Conversations (LRC) is to **build connection, one conversation at a time**. The organization is explicitly working to create opportunities for Americans to converse together with **mutual respect and civility** regardless of differences.

## Methodology

A Living Room Conversation is an event where people come together in person or online to discuss an issue using **easy-to-use "conversation guides"**. These guides are designed to help everyone feel like they can join the conversation and foster **connection and understanding**.

## Resources

LRC provides conversation guides on **more than 150 topics** including:

- Politics, Polarization, and Government
- Community: Relationships and Social Connection
- Culture and Society
- Values (American Values, Hope)
- Post Election: Moving Forward Together

Featured starter guides include:
- *Conversations in Troubled Times*
- *Peace Building in the United States*
- *American Values*
- *Listening Courageously*
