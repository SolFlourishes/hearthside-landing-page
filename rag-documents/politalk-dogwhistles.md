# Dogwhistles: Political Manipulation Through Coded Language

## Definition

A dogwhistle utterance allows a candidate to send a message to one portion of the electorate that other portions might find alienating. The modern focus is on a kind of **deliberate manipulation** by politicians designed to be unnoticed by most of the public.

## Types of Dogwhistles

### Intentional Overt Dogwhistles

An overt intentional dogwhistle is a speech act designed, with intent, to allow **two plausible interpretations**. One interpretation is a **private, coded message** targeted for a subset of the general audience, concealed so that the general audience remains unaware of the existence of the coded interpretation. The use of a code phrase provides the speaker with **deniability**.

**Examples:**
- **"Wonder-working power":** **Fundamentalist Christians** hear a reference to the **power of Christ**, signaling group membership
- **"Dred Scott":** The **anti-abortion right** recognizes this as opposition to **abortion**

### Covert Intentional Dogwhistles

These play a special role in American race discourse, particularly since the 1960s, due to the widely accepted **Norm of Racial Equality**.

**Mechanism:** A covert dogwhistle is powerful because people **fail to consciously recognize it**. It appears innocuous and unrelated to race, offering deniability if confronted. The dogwhistled content **influences the audience's voting decisions without the audience being aware of it**.

**Examples:**
- **Willie Horton Ad:** Strong exposure increased the likelihood of **racially resentful voters** favoring Bush
- **"Inner City":** Functions as a dogwhistle for **"black"**
- **"Welfare":** Can erode respect by carrying "not-at-issue" content that certain groups are lazy

## Combating Dogwhistles

1. **Conscious Defusion:** **Explicitly raising the issue of race can defuse a racial dogwhistle**
2. **Complicating the Narrative:** Feature nuance, contradiction, and ambiguity rather than simple pro vs con
3. **Counter-Stereotypical Imagery:** Include **counter-stereotypical imagery** to undermine dogwhistles
4. **Talk to the Elephant:** Appeal to intuition first rather than reason alone
