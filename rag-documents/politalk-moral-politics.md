# Moral Politics: How Liberals and Conservatives Think

## The Worldview Divide

Contemporary American politics centers on **worldview**, where conservatives and liberals conceptualize the world differently. Political stands on issues are generally **not random or arbitrary**; they follow from one's underlying moral worldview.

### The Cognitive Basis

- **Unconscious Thought:** Most thought is unconscious (est. 98%), and moral worldviews are typically unconscious
- **Metaphorical Thought:** A **conceptual metaphor** is a correspondence between concepts across domains
- **The Worldview Problem:** If facts do not fit a deeply held, neural-wired worldview, they may be ignored, changed, rejected, or attacked
- **Politics as Morality:** Politics is fundamentally about **morality**—not just policy or issue-by-issue debate

## The Two Family Models

The political divide rests on two distinct, idealized models of the family, linked to political life by the **Nation As Family metaphor**.

### Strict Father Morality (Conservative Worldview)

The Strict Father model sees the world divided into **good and evil**. The father's role is to support, protect, and guide. Children are initially morally weak and must be **disciplined** to gain strength and self-reliance.

**Core Metaphors:**
- **Moral Strength:** Morality is strength; immorality is weakness. Punishment/hardship builds strength
- **Moral Authority:** Legitimate authority must be upheld at all costs
- **Moral Order:** Links dominance to moral authority (the most moral should rule)
- **Moral Essence:** Character is **fixed** by significant past actions
- **Moral Purity:** Immorality is impurity that can spread and must be purged
- **Moral Self-Interest:** Seeking one's self-interest is a moral activity

### Nurturant Parent Morality (Liberal Worldview)

This model is based on **nurturance**. The goal is to maximize the development of human potential, which requires empathy and fairness.

**Core Metaphors:**
- **Morality As Nurturance:** Helping people in need is moral. Stresses interdependence and **social responsibility**
- **Morality As Empathy:** Putting oneself in others' shoes; a moral activity that leads to nurturance
- **Morality As Happiness:** Creates an anti-ascetic morality where sensuality is a virtue
- **Morality As Self-Development:** Development of human potential is a moral calling
- **Morality As Fair Distribution:** Brings issues of equality and equitability into the moral system
- **Moral Growth:** Moral character is **capable of growth and change**

## Implications for Communication

- Policy positions are unified by their respective family-based moral systems
- Public political discourse is **impoverished** and lacks the necessary moral vocabulary
- **Traditional debate** and objective news reporting are inadequate because they assume issues are isolatable and concepts are literal and neutral
- Conservatives have a significant advantage in discourse because they have successfully designed language and slogans that reinforce the **conceptual links** between their moral values
- Liberals often fail by relying on an outdated Enlightenment view of literal, rational debate
