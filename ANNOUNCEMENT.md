# 🎉 Introducing Clarity Coach Beta 4.1: Your Personal Communication Guide

We're excited to announce **Clarity Coach Beta 4.1** - a research-based AI tool designed to help you bridge communication gaps, understand different perspectives, and build authentic connections.

## 🌟 What is Clarity Coach?

Clarity Coach is a neurodiversity-affirming communication tool grounded in research on autism, ADHD, the Double Empathy Problem, and cross-cultural communication. Whether you're navigating workplace dynamics, family relationships, or political conversations, Clarity Coach helps you:

✨ **Draft messages** that match your intent and your audience's communication style
🔍 **Analyze messages** you've received to understand the likely intent behind the words
💬 **Chat with the Coach** for real-time advice on tricky conversations
🗳️ **Explore Political Worldviews** to understand the moral frameworks behind divisive positions

## 🚀 What's New in Beta 4.0 & 4.1?

### Beta 4.0: User Accounts & Personalization
- **Create Your Account** - Save your communication preferences (neurotype, generation, style)
- **Communication Style Quiz** - Discover your archetype (Analyzer, Harmonizer, Strategist, Advocate, or Adapter)
- **Auto-Populated Context** - Your profile automatically loads in Draft, Analyze, and Chat modes
- **Visual Indicators** - Clear badges show when your saved preferences are being used
- **Avatar Upload** - Personalize your profile with custom avatars

### Beta 4.1: PoliTalk Explorer & Research Tools
- **PoliTalk Explorer** - Go beyond message translation to understand WHY people believe what they believe
- **Topic Library** - Browse 20+ curated political topics organized by category
- **Moral Framework Analysis** - Explore how different value systems create internally consistent but conflicting viewpoints
- **Granular Political Identities** - Select from Progressive, Liberal, Moderate, Conservative, Libertarian plus specific values
- **Translation Consistency Tester** (Admin) - Research tool validating semantic preservation across translation cycles

## 🎯 Our Mission

Clarity Coach is built on three core principles:

1. **Hear Me** - Ensure your literal intent is accurately received

3. **Know Me** - Foster authentic connection through better understanding
